Market Commentary

Week ending March 28, 2026

The S&P/TSX Composite gained 1.5% to close at 25,427, outperforming the S&P 500 (+0.8%) for the third consecutive week — resource-heavy sectors led the advance, with the TSX Materials sub-index up 3.2% on rising gold and copper prices. WTI crude traded in a $67–$71 range before settling at $69.40, while the Canadian dollar weakened 0.4% against the U.S. dollar to 0.7198.

The Government of Canada 10-year yield rose 8 basis points to 3.58%, widening the 2s10s spread to 63 basis points — the yield curve remained positively sloped for the fourth consecutive month. The Bank of Canada held its overnight rate at 3.25% at its March 12 decision; the next announcement is April 16.

Project cross-reference: The database tracks 94 mining and oil & gas projects ($62B) sensitive to commodity price movements. Of these, 12 Alberta oil sands projects ($18.2B) have breakeven costs above the current WTI level. 42 power and energy projects ($19B) are linked to natural gas pricing.
