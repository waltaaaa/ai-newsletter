Industry Overview

20 NAICS sectors · GDP by industry, January 2026

Fourteen of 20 NAICS sectors recorded positive month-over-month GDP growth in January — led by utilities (+1.1%), construction (+0.8%), and arts & recreation (+0.6%). The six contracting sectors were concentrated in resource extraction and trade, with mining and oil & gas posting the steepest decline at −1.2%.

Pipeline cross-reference: The database tracks 1,247 active projects ($418B) across all sectors. Goods-producing sectors account for 378 projects ($195B), services-producing sectors 869 projects ($223B). 34 projects changed status this week.
