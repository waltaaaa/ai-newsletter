## GOODS-PRODUCING INDUSTRIES

### NAICS 11 — Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing & Hunting
- Canola production hit a record 21.8 million tonnes for 2025-26, 13% above last year and 19% above the five-year average. Export projection raised to 8.2 Mt (vs. 9.3 Mt last year). ([Agriculture Canada](https://agriculture.canada.ca/en/sector/crops/reports-statistics/canada-outlook-principal-field-crops-2026-03-18))
- Canada-China canola agreement: combined tariffs to drop from ~85% to ~15% by March 1, 2026 on a $4-billion market. ([Seed World](https://www.seedworld.com/canada/2026/01/16/canada-secures-major-canola-and-agriculture-trade-breakthrough-in-new-china-partnership/))
- Total Canadian grain production reached a record 107 million tonnes in 2025. Carry-out stocks projected to increase 67% due to a 3% decline in exports. ([FCC](https://www.fcc-fac.ca/en/knowledge/2026-crop-outlook))
- Federal government investing $75 million over 5 years (2026-31) in AgriMarketing Program for market diversification. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/agriculture-agri-food/news/2026/02/the-government-of-canada-strengthens-support-for-agricultural-exports-with-a-new-market-diversification-program.html))
- Prices for most principal field crops forecast lower year-over-year, except soybeans and mustard seed.

### NAICS 21 — Mining, Oil & Gas Extraction
- CAOEC projects 213 active drilling rigs in 2026 (up from 201 in 2025), with 5,709 wells expected (+3%). ([Canadian Energy Centre](https://www.canadianenergycentre.ca/five-things-to-watch-in-canadas-oil-and-gas-industry-in-2026/))
- Federal government announced $3.6 billion in new critical minerals programs. $165.2 million for 22 projects unlocking $434 million across 8 provinces. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2026/03/government-of-canada-invests-to-unlock-canadas-critical-minerals-advantage.html))
- Critical Minerals Partnerships Alliance mobilizing $18.5 billion in Canadian projects (combining $12.1B second-round + $6.4B October 2025 round). ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2026/03/canada-secures-30-new-critical-minerals-partnerships-and-unlocks-121-billion-in-mining-project-capital.html))
- Fast-tracked projects include Foran Mining McIlvenna Bay copper-zinc (SK) and Red Chris copper mine expansion (BC, +15% annual copper production). ([MINING.COM](https://www.mining.com/canada-launches-fast-track-reviews-for-major-mines-and-energy-projects/))
- Indigenous-led floating LNG terminal on BC's northern coast nearing FID, expected in 2026.
- Critical Minerals Sovereign Fund: $2 billion over 5 years; First and Last Mile Fund: $1.5 billion over 4 years starting 2026-27. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2026/03/backgrounder-government-of-canada-invests-to-unlock-canadas-critical-minerals-advantage.html))
- Canada is fourth-largest cobalt producer globally; four new cobalt mines under development in BC, QC, NB, and NT.
- Mangrove Lithium spodumene processing plant in Delta, BC received conditional approval ($21.9M funding).

### NAICS 22 — Utilities
- $28.9 million in federal funding for 12 clean energy projects (CCUS, renewables, smart grid). ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2026/03/backgrounder-canada-invests-in-energy-innovation-to-become-a-clean-energy-superpower.html))
- Canadian Renewable Energy Association expects installed wind, solar, and storage capacity to grow by one-third in four years, double in a decade. ~24 GW of opportunities announced; ~8 GW expected online by 2029. ([CanREA](https://renewablesassociation.ca/news-release-the-stage-is-set-for-the-future-of-canadas-wind-solar-and-energy-storage-industry/))
- Ontario LT2 battery energy storage system (BESS) awards expected Q2 2026. Quebec and BC procuring thousands of MW of renewables (tripling QC wind/solar, quadrupling BC). ([ConstructConnect](https://canada.constructconnect.com/dcn/news/resource/2026/03/canada-energy-regulator-projects-power-generation-surge-with-wind-a-major-new-source))
- Canada Energy Regulator projects power generation to increase 30%-100% by 2050, driven partly by AI data centre demand.
- Darlington SMR: OPG applied to CNSC for Licence to Operate in March 2026. Basemat placement scheduled Summer 2026. Four BWRX-300 units (1,200 MW total) estimated at $20.9 billion. ([OPG](https://www.opg.com/projects-services/projects/nuclear/smr/darlington-smr/))
- SaskPower BWRX-300 deployment targeting mid-2030s; construction decision expected 2029.

### NAICS 23 — Construction
- February 2026 building permits: $12.1 billion (-8.4% MoM). Non-residential collapsed 24.0% to $4.0B (institutional -51.5%, commercial -7.2%, industrial -9.6%). ([StatCan](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260413/dq260413b-eng.htm))
- Residential: 21,000 multi-unit + 3,900 single-family dwellings authorized in February (-0.8% MoM). 12-month multi-family total 255,500 (up from 244,600 prior period).
- Civil construction is the only major category projected for growth in 2026, with ~30% expansion expected. ([On-Site Magazine](https://www.on-sitemag.com/features/2026-forecast-canadas-construction-crossroads/))
- Federal budget commitments: $25B housing, $30B defence/security, $110B productivity/competitiveness, $115B infrastructure.
- Build Communities Strong Fund: $51 billion over 10 years launched April 7, 2026. First tranche: 13 projects, $300M federal. ([PM.gc.ca](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/04/07/prime-minister-carney-launches-build-communities-strong-fund-and))

### NAICS 31-33 — Manufacturing
- Canadian auto production expected to decline ~4% in 2026; 2025 production was 1.2M+ vehicles (-5.4% YoY). ([TD Economics](https://economics.td.com/ca-auto-outlook-update))
- Auto exports fell 21.2% to $5.4B in January 2026 (lowest since Sep 2021), then recovered +30.2% in February. ([BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/economics/2026/03/12/canadas-auto-exports-fall-to-multi-year-low/))
- Government announced $3B Strategic Response Fund + $100M Regional Tariff Response Initiative for auto sector. Goal: 75% EV sales by 2035, 90% by 2040. ([PM.gc.ca](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/02/05/prime-minister-carney-launches-new-strategy-transform-canadas-auto))
- VW PowerCo (St. Thomas): completed primary concrete foundations; will be Canada's largest manufacturing plant. NextStar Energy (Windsor): commenced battery module production. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2026/04/backgrounder-government-of-canada-invests-in-electric-vehicles.html))
- $11.7M for 15 EV charging projects installing 1,600+ chargers nationally.

## SERVICE INDUSTRIES

### NAICS 41 — Wholesale Trade
- Motor vehicle and parts wholesale sales rose 6.1% to $14.4B in February 2026. Food/beverage/tobacco +2.9% to $16.5B. Personal/household goods +2.5% to record $13.2B. ([StatCan](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260415/dq260415b-eng.htm))
- Supply chain realignments occurring in automotive, life sciences, and advanced materials sectors. 75% of Canadian exports are US-destined.

### NAICS 44-45 — Retail Trade
- January 2026 retail sales: $70.7B (+1.1% MoM), up in 6 of 9 subsectors. February advance estimate: +0.9%. ([StatCan](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260320/dq260320a-eng.htm))
- Q1 2026 total spending declined 0.27% YoY per Moneris. Grocery +3% YoY, mass merchants +7%. Apparel -2%, household -2%, department stores -8%. ([Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2026/04/soft-start-to-2026-masks-resilient-consumer-spending-in-canada/))
- 43% of Canadians plan to reduce non-essential spending; only 13% expect economic improvement in next 6 months.

### NAICS 48-49 — Transportation & Warehousing
- Canada Freight & Logistics market: USD $116.63B in 2026, CAGR 4.45% to $145.05B by 2031. ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/canada-freight-logistics-market-study))
- Port of Montreal Contrecoeur terminal expansion: +1.15M TEUs annual capacity (~60% of current throughput). CIB $1.6B loan. ([RT&S](https://www.rtands.com/ports/canadian-infrastructure-bank-supports-montreal-port-authority-expansion-with-1-6-billion-loan/))
- Road freight: 60.97% of market. Air freight CAGR 5.15% (2026-31).
- Federal plan to cut interprovincial freight rates for Canadian steel and lumber starting Spring 2026.

### NAICS 51 — Information & Cultural Industries
- Canada Telecom MNO market: USD $52.28B in 2026, CAGR 4.65% to $65.59B by 2031. ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/canada-telecom-market))
- $64B+ in network investment over past 5 years. 5G coverage exceeds 85% of population. CRTC target: 100% 4G LTE+ coverage by 2026. ([CRTC](https://www.crtc.gc.ca/eng/publications/reports/PolicyMonitoring/2026/ctmr.htm))
- Revenue and subscriber growth slowing as population growth decelerates. Operators moderating network investment.
- AI Sovereign Compute Infrastructure Program: ~$890M over 7 years starting 2026-27 for supercomputing. Applications close June 1, 2026. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/04/canada-launches-national-initiative-to-build-large-scale-ai-supercomputing-capacity.html))
- Microsoft: $19B Canada commitment (2023-2027) for cloud/AI infrastructure. ([Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/04/07/building-ai-infrastructure-the-community-first-way-in-canada/))

### NAICS 52 — Finance & Insurance
- Big Six banks Q1 2026 (ended Jan 31): all beat analyst estimates. RBC record net income $5.8B (+13% YoY). TD $4.04B (+45%). CIBC $3.1B. BMO $2.5B. National Bank $1.25B. ([Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-canada-banks-first-quarter-results-earnings-2026/))
- Wealth management and capital markets driving profits. 2026 is the final year of 5-year mortgage renewal cycle from 2021. ([BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/investing/market-outlook/2026/02/24/market-outlook-canadian-banks-kick-off-earnings-with-profits-tied-to-stocks/))
- P&C insurers face claims inflation pressure, particularly auto insurance. OSFI flags softening commercial lines. ([OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/reports-publications/osfis-annual-risk-outlook-fiscal-year-2026-2027))

### NAICS 53 — Real Estate & Rental/Leasing
- National office vacancy: 13.6% in Q1 2026 (down 1pp YoY). Industrial vacancy: 3.5% (first national decline since 2022). First simultaneous office+industrial vacancy decline since 2020. ([CP24](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/04/20/commercial-real-estate-market-at-turning-point-as-vacancies-drop-report/))
- CRE investment forecast: ~$56B in 2026 (+8% from ~$47B in 2025). 97% of market participants expect activity to increase or remain stable. ([CBRE](https://www.cbre.ca/insights/articles/cbre-outlook-canadian-commercial-real-estate-investment-could-rise-to-56-billion-dollar-in-2026))
- Industrial net absorption forecast to rebound to 20M+ sq ft in 2026 (pre-pandemic norms).
- 2025 was the first year with more purpose-built rentals under construction than condos or single-family homes. Asking rents falling in every major city except Edmonton. ([Altus Group](https://www.altusgroup.com/insights/what-regional-data-reveals-about-canadas-housing-outlook-for-2026/))

### NAICS 54 — Professional, Scientific & Technical Services
- AI adoption, cybersecurity, data management, and digital transformation driving consulting demand. Toronto is the largest consulting hub. ([Fasken](https://www.fasken.com/en/knowledge/2026/03/navigating-2026-key-trends-shaping-tech-sector))
- Energy, finance, healthcare, and technology sectors driving consulting growth for digital transformation and sustainability strategies.

### NAICS 55 — Management of Companies & Enterprises
- Brookfield Corporation: ~US$900B AUM. Onex: ~CA$50B. OMERS Private Equity: C$23.2B net PE assets. Northleaf: ~CA$28B. ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/investor-lists/private-equity-funds-in-canada/__KWH6B4ICxuUDDMNrgCJaeOHSp_H5scpAHX8Z5vs0QH8))
- 982 private equity funds in Canada; Toronto (417) and Vancouver (157) host the most. Collective investments exceed $1.94 trillion across 5,300+ companies.

### NAICS 56 — Administrative & Support, Waste Management
- Canada Waste Management market: USD $37.02B (2023), projected $64.22B by 2030 (CAGR 7.10%). ([NextMSC](https://www.nextmsc.com/report/canada-waste-management-market))
- GFL Environmental acquiring SECURE Waste Infrastructure at $24.75/share (23% premium to 60-day VWAP), expanding western Canadian footprint. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/gfl-environmental-and-secure-waste-infrastructure-announce-acquisition-by-gfl-further-expanding-and-densifying-gfls-western-canadian-footprint-302740366.html))
- Ontario extended producer responsibility for blue box services fully in effect as of January 1, 2026.

### NAICS 61 — Educational Services
- Ontario: $6.4B additional higher education funding over 4 years (effective Fall 2026), adding 70,000 seats for in-demand programs. ([McMaster News](https://news.mcmaster.ca/ontario-announces-postsecondary-education-funding-feb-2026/))
- Federal international student caps caused 48% drop in first-semester college enrollment in Ontario in 2025. Financial strain: operating deficits ~$338M in 2024-25, projected ~$265M shortfall in 2025-26. ([University Affairs](https://universityaffairs.ca/news/the-year-ahead-in-higher-ed/))
- York University suspended enrollment in 18 programs (mostly arts/humanities). Queen's reallocated 1,200 spots from BA to STEM/professional programs.

### NAICS 62 — Health Care & Social Assistance
- Ontario 2026 budget: $1.1B+ for hospitals, $9B+ for long-term care, $64B toward 10-year health infrastructure plan. Additional $1.1B over 3 years for home/community care. ([Ontario Budget](https://budget.ontario.ca/2026/chapter-1b-services.html))
- 147 LTC projects (23,977 new/redeveloped beds) completed, under construction, or approved in Ontario.
- BC halted construction of 7 LTC facilities (Abbotsford, Campbell River, Chilliwack, Delta, Fort St. John, Kelowna, Squamish). Work underway on 30 hospitals, 11 LTC centres, 4 cancer centres. ([The Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/02/20/BC-Halts-Construction-Seven-Long-Term-Care-Facilities/))
- South Niagara Hospital: final structural beam raised Feb 2026; 469 beds, completion summer 2028.

### NAICS 71 — Arts, Entertainment & Recreation
- Canada Council for the Arts: grants up to $100,000/year (3-year max) for composite activities; up to $30,000 for travel/promotion. ([Canada Council](https://canadacouncil.ca/))
- OAC Multi/Inter-Arts: up to $20,000. Quebec prescribing free concerts as health intervention.
- GDP data for the sector available via StatCan and ISED industry statistics. ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/app/ixb/cis/summary-sommaire/71))

### NAICS 72 — Accommodation & Food Services
- Hospitality market: USD $21.34B in 2026, CAGR 5.18% to $27.46B by 2031. ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/hospitality-industry-in-canada))
- Hotel sector: record occupancy 65.7%, ADR CAD $208.71, RevPAR CAD $137.17 (+28% vs pre-pandemic) in 2024. CoStar projects 1.9% RevPAR growth in 2026 (vs 0.6% for US). ([Cushman & Wakefield](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/canada/insights/canadian-lodging-industry-overview))
- 332 hotel projects (45,429 rooms) in pipeline as of end 2025. Restaurant sales crossed CAD $120B for the first time.
- Labour shortages: industry advocating 33,000 permanent residency spots for TFWs. FIFA 2026 is a key tourism driver.

### NAICS 81 — Other Services (Repair & Maintenance)
- Employment in "other services" declined 14,000 (-1.8%) in February 2026. ([StatCan](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260313/dq260313a-eng.htm))
- 500,000+ Canadians employed in auto care industry. National right-to-repair legislation under discussion. ([Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/markets-news/GlobeNewswire/40110/new-report-shows-right-to-repair-could-save-drivers-up-to-500-on-common-repairs/))
- Apple expanded Self Service Repair program to Canada in August 2025.

### NAICS 91 — Public Administration
- 2026-27 Main Estimates: $502.8B total budgetary spending ($230.4B voted, $272.4B statutory). ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/planned-government-spending/government-expenditure-plan-main-estimates/2026-27-estimates.html))
- Increases in elderly benefits, Canada Health Transfer, fiscal equalization. Winding down of federal fuel charge rebates (removed April 1, 2025).
- Defence spending increased to 2% of GDP (~$63B in 2025-26), targeting 5% by 2035. Defence Industrial Strategy mandates 70% domestic procurement. Projected: 125,000 careers, 50% export growth, 240% industry revenue growth. ([PM.gc.ca](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/02/17/prime-minister-carney-launches-canadas-first-defence-industrial))
- Canada joined EU SAFE military procurement program ($243B fund) -- first non-EU member.

## CROSS-SECTOR THEMES

### Major Project Acceleration
- Build Communities Strong Fund: $51B over 10 years ($17.2B provincial/territorial, $6B direct delivery, $27.8B community stream + $3B/yr ongoing). First tranche: 13 projects, $300M (Apr 7, 2026). ([PM.gc.ca](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/04/07/prime-minister-carney-launches-build-communities-strong-fund-and))
- Manitoba co-operation agreement signed Apr 14 for "one project, one review" approach. Alberta co-operation agreement signed Apr 2. Nova Scotia signed March 27. ([PM.gc.ca](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/04/14/canada-and-manitoba-sign-new-agreement-get-major-projects-built))

### LNG Export Terminals
- LNG Canada Phase 1 (14 MTPA): Train 1 first cargo June 2025, Train 2 November 2025, ramping to full capacity in 2026. Phase 2 FID expected by end of 2026 (would double to 28 MTPA). ([Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/10bef30f/canadian-lng-industry-2026-outlook))
- Woodfibre LNG (0.3 Bcf/d): exports expected 2027. Cedar LNG (0.4 Bcf/d): FID June 2024, exports expected 2028.

### EV Battery Supply Chain
- VW PowerCo (St. Thomas): primary foundations complete, will be Canada's largest manufacturing plant. NextStar Energy (Windsor): battery module production commenced. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2026/04/backgrounder-government-of-canada-invests-in-electric-vehicles.html))
- Domestic lithium refineries opening in 2026 reduce overseas processing reliance.
- Some projects facing construction pauses, cancellations, and reevaluations.

### Data Centre & AI Infrastructure
- Federal AI Sovereign Compute Infrastructure Program: ~$890M over 7 years. Applications for large-scale (100+ MW) sovereign data centres close June 1, 2026. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/04/canada-launches-national-initiative-to-build-large-scale-ai-supercomputing-capacity.html))
- Microsoft: $19B Canada commitment (2023-2027) for cloud/AI infrastructure. ([Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/04/07/building-ai-infrastructure-the-community-first-way-in-canada/))
- Hyperscale data centre cost: ~$10M/MW construction + $30M/MW compute infrastructure.

### Indigenous Economic Development
- Indigenous Loan Guarantee Program doubled from $5B to $10B, expanded beyond energy/natural resources. Aamjiwnaang/Chippewas acquiring ~20% equity in Chatham-to-Lakeshore transmission project. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/03/government-supports-equity-purchase-of-hydro-one-line-to-advance-indigenous-economic-participation.html))
- $850,000+ for 14 Indigenous projects in Northern Ontario tied to critical minerals. First and Last Mile Fund: $1.5B over 4 years starting 2026-27. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/prairies-economic-development/news/2026/02/government-of-canada-to-announce-federal-support-for-indigenous-economic-development.html))
- Northern Horizons program: training 1,200 Indigenous/northern women in digital skills (YT, NT, NU).
