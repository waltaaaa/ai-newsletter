## ONTARIO

**Unemployment:** 7.6% (March 2026, unchanged MoM). Projected to decrease to 7.4% by end of 2026.

**GDP:** Real GDP grew an estimated 1.2% in 2025 (up from 0.8% forecast). Projected average annual real GDP growth of 1.4% from 2025-2028.

**Budget 2026 ("A Plan to Protect Ontario"):** Tabled Mar 26 by Finance Minister Peter Bethlenfalvy. Deficit: $12.3B in 2025-26 and $13.8B in 2026-27. Balance projected for 2028-29 (one year later than previous projection -- third consecutive extension). 10-year capital plan of $210B+, including $37B in 2026-27.

**Key Legislation -- Bill 98 (Building Homes and Improving Transportation Infrastructure Act):** Announced Mar 30. Development charges reduced 30-50% for priority municipalities over 3 years with federal-provincial cost-matched $8.8B over 10 years. HST rebate for new homes up to $1M (subject to federal legislation). Planning simplification measures. Municipalities restricted from imposing green building standards beyond provincial code.

**Housing Partnership:** Federal-Ontario agreement announced Mar 30 to cut taxes on housing and boost supply. Projected $2.2B in tax relief, 8,000 additional housing starts, up to 21,000 jobs, $2.7B GDP contribution. Housing starts projected to moderate from 65,400 (2025) to 64,800 (2026).

**Major Project:** Kinross Gold Great Bear Project (Red Lake, Northwestern Ontario): $5B+ capital investment, up to 1,100 operational jobs.

**Tariff Response:** Budget focused on protecting Ontario from U.S. tariff impacts on automotive, steel, and forestry sectors.

Sources: https://budget.ontario.ca/2026/contents.html, https://budget.ontario.ca/2026/chapter-1b-economy.html, https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/03/30/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnership-ontario-cut-taxes, https://fao-on.org/en/report/2026-ontario-budget-note/, https://globalnews.ca/news/11751929/ontario-housing-transit-bill-2026/, https://news.ontario.ca/en/release/1006645/province-introducing-legislation-to-build-homes-and-infrastructure-faster

## ALBERTA

**Unemployment:** 6.3% (two-year low). Approximately 85,000 jobs added over the past year.

**GDP:** Real GDP growth projected at 2.7% in 2026 (up from Dec forecast of 2.1%). Nominal GDP +6%.

**Budget 2026:** Deficit of C$9.4B for 2026-27. Non-renewable resource revenues estimated at $13.2B (18% of total revenues), down $8.8B from 2024-25 due to lower WTI and wider WTI-WCS differential.

**Carbon Pricing:** Alberta MOU with Ottawa commits to $130/tonne industrial carbon price (up 37% from $95) effective Apr 1, 2026. However, Alberta froze TIER fund credits at $95/tonne indefinitely in May 2025. Direct Investment Pathway (DIP) set to launch in 2026. Alberta Carbon Capture Incentive Program (ACCIP) projects $35B in new investment and 21,000 jobs over next decade.

**Energy Sector:** Record oil production concurrent with blue hydrogen and CCUS scaling. Data centre proposals requesting 20+ GW of power as province targets $100B in data centre investment.

**Key Projects in Pipeline Database:** Edmonton-Calgary High Speed Rail (EllisDon/AECOM), Alberta Rural Broadband Deployment ($374M), multiple infrastructure projects under IAAC review.

Sources: https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/atb-alberta-oil-gas-economy-forecast-9.7143848, https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-alberta-budget-2026-oil-prices-energy-forecast-deficit/, https://weeklyvoice.com/alberta-energy-2026-record-oil-output-blue-hydrogen-expansion-and-the-ai-power-boom/, https://www.alberta.ca/technology-innovation-and-emissions-reduction-regulation, https://www.alberta.ca/alberta-carbon-capture-incentive-program

## BRITISH COLUMBIA

**Unemployment:** 6.7% (March 2026, up 0.6 pp MoM -- highest since Feb 2016).

**Budget 2026:** Tabled Feb 17, 2026.

**LNG Projects:** Ksi Lisims LNG designed as one of the world's lowest-emission LNG operations (emissions 94% below global average once electrified). ~$30B investment expected. Proposed LNG projects could add 28.5 million tonnes/year by end of decade. North Coast Transmission Line (NCTL) legislation introduced; construction planned to begin 2026.

**Mining:** Two mines approved in early 2026. Western Canadian Critical Minerals Strategy signed Jan 25. BC hosts 16 of 34 federally identified critical minerals. 27 proposed mines could generate $90B near-term and $984B long-term economic activity. 2025 was BC's strongest exploration year on record (spending up 36% YoY).

**Mining Permits Reform (Apr 1, 2026):** New processing timelines -- 40 to 140 days depending on complexity. Three-stream application processing with fixed timelines. Escalation process: if timelines missed, chief permitting officer decides within 14 days. $1M budget lift for staff, $2M for Mineral Claims Consultation Framework.

**Housing & Transit:** 116 new below-market rental homes in Burnaby Metrotown (near SkyTrain), ~$20M provincial grant via BC Builds. BC Transit receiving $189M over 10 years (CPTF). Target: 10,000 homes near transit hubs over 10-15 years. Federal funding announced for 100+ homes in BC.

Sources: https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026MCM0003-000071, https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/look-west-strategy/delivering-major-projects-faster, https://www.cedarlng.com/, https://thenarwhal.ca/bc-mining-push-2026/, https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/media-newsroom/news-releases/2026/canada-announces-funding-over-100-homes-bc, https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0018-000177

## NEWFOUNDLAND AND LABRADOR

**Unemployment:** 9.5% (March 2026, up 0.3 pp MoM -- highest among provinces).

**Offshore Oil Production:** February 2026: 8.7 million barrels (up 69.2% YoY). Production value: $837.2M (up 51.7% YoY). Brent crude surpassed $120/bbl in late March before easing to ~$110.

**Bay du Nord Agreement (Mar 3, 2026):** Life-of-field benefits, royalties, and equity option agreed with Equinor and BP. First new standalone offshore development since Hebron. First deepwater project. $14B estimated investment. Up to $6.4B in direct provincial revenue (Phase 1). First oil expected 2031. FPSO-based subsea tieback design.

**Economic Significance:** Offshore activity averaged ~$8.9B/year in GDP over 2018-2024 (23.3% of provincial GDP).

**Defence:** Federal defence investments announced at 5 Wing Goose Bay (Apr 1, 2026).

## Key Themes This Week

1. **Federal infrastructure spending surge:** $51B Build Communities Strong Fund launched Apr 7, with provincial cost-matching requirements. One-project-one-review agreements being signed (Manitoba Apr 14).

2. **Housing acceleration:** Federal-Ontario ($8.8B), Federal-Quebec ($200M affordable homes), Federal-BC (100+ homes) partnerships all active. Bill 98 in Ontario reduces development charges 30-50%.

3. **Energy transition tensions:** Alberta $130/tonne industrial carbon commitment vs. $95 TIER freeze. Consumer carbon tax eliminated. Federal fuel excise suspended Apr 14-Sep 7.

4. **Resource sector momentum:** Saskatchewan uranium (2 new mines, first since 2004), BC mining permits reform (Apr 1), Yukon Kudz Ze Kayah zinc mine approved, NL Bay du Nord $14B offshore deal.

5. **Fiscal pressures:** Ontario deficit $13.8B, Alberta deficit $9.4B, PEI deficit $410M (3.5% of GDP, debt approaching 40% of GDP). Trade uncertainty from U.S. tariffs cited across multiple budgets.

6. **Defence and sovereignty:** NL 5 Wing Goose Bay federal investment, NB Premier pressing for defence industrial strategy, Alberta Sovereignty Act framework active.
